Our story

It started with one grill and twelve chairs

In 2014, chef Rafael Ocampo traded a white-tablecloth kitchen for a converted warehouse and a single oak-fired grill. The menu had twelve dishes, the room had twelve chairs, and by winter there was a waitlist for all of them.

The idea never changed: cook everything over live fire, buy from people we know by name, and treat service like hosting friends rather than serving customers. A decade later the room is bigger — the grill is still the heart of it.

  • Every dish touches oak, ember or smoke
  • Producers within 120 km supply 80% of our ingredients
  • Zero-waste kitchen since 2021

What we believe

Three things we never compromise

They shape every plate, pour and hello at the door.

Fire-first cooking

No gas, no shortcuts. Oak embers, live flame and smoke build the flavor that defines every dish we serve.

Seasonal & local

The menu changes with the fields and the coast around us. If it isn't in season within 120 km, it isn't on the plate.

Hospitality first

Names remembered, allergies noted, celebrations quietly planned. The warm room is as much our recipe as anything we cook.
